## 📱 Mobile App Features
1. **Full 37-Product Catalog**: Instant search by English, Tamil (மரச்செக்கு தேங்காய் எண்ணெய், etc.), or SKU code.
2. **Category Tabs**: Cold-Pressed Oils, Grains & Millets, Spices & Masalas, Cattle & Animal Feed, By-Products, Premium Rice.
3. **Mandatory 10-Digit Mobile Number**: Bills cannot be processed without customer phone number.
4. **WhatsApp Bill Sharing**: 1-Tap sharing to customer WhatsApp with formatted receipt details and Tamil greeting (*நன்றி! மீண்டும் வருக!*).
5. **Thermal Print Support**: Direct printing formatted for 58mm & 80mm ESC/POS roll printers.
6. **Payment Modes**: UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), CASH, CREDIT.
7. **Offline PWA Support**: Works seamlessly without internet connection.
8. **Role Authentication**:
   - **Cashier Mode** (PIN `5678`): Fast counter billing & receipt generation.
   - **CEO Mode** (PIN `1234`): Sales ledger audit & business summary.
