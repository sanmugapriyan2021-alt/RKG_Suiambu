3. BILLING WORKFLOW & OPERATION
================================================================================
Step 1: SELECT CATEGORY & PRODUCTS
   * Click any category tab (Cattle Feed, Cold-Pressed Oil, Raw Grains, Millets, Organic).
   * Click on any product tile (e.g. Pure Groundnut Oil 1L, Cattle Feed 50kg).
   * The item is immediately added to the Cart on the right.

Step 2: CART EDITING
   * In the cart, review the Description, Quantity, and Price.
   * To change quantity: Select the product in the cart, and click "+1 QTY" or "-1 QTY" at the bottom.
   * To remove a product: Select the item and click "🗑️ DELETE SELECTED".

Step 3: CUSTOMER INFORMATION
   * Customer Name  : Enter customer's name (1st column).
   * Mobile Number  : Enter 10-digit mobile number (2nd column).
   * Payment Mode   : Choose UPI (GPay/PhonePe), Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit.

Step 4: APPLY PROMO CODE (OPTIONAL)
   * Enter active promo code (e.g. DISC5, RKG10, FARMER15, SUYAMBU20, AGRO25, PONGAL30)
   * Click "Apply". The discount is automatically calculated and capped to maximum rule limits.

Step 5: PRINT THERMAL RECEIPT & COMPLETE
   * Click the large green "🖨️ PRINT 80MM BILL & CHARGE" button (or press Enter).
   * The 80mm thermal receipt is immediately printed to your TVS RP 3200 printer.

================================================================================
4. OFFLINE SQL DATABASE & AUTOMATIC CLOUD RECONNECTION
================================================================================
* OFFLINE MODE: If the internet or cloud connection drops, the billing station 
  NEVER STOPS. Every bill is immediately saved to the local SQL database 
  (`pos_offline_ledger.sql`) with status 'PENDING_OFFLINE'.
* AUTOMATIC RECONNECT: As soon as the network returns, the system automatically 
  reads all pending bills from the offline SQL ledger, syncs them to Firebase 
  Firestore (`bill_numbers`, `daily_transfer_items`, `invoices`), and updates 
  their status to 'SYNCED_ONLINE'.

================================================================================
5. HARDWARE & THERMAL PRINTER SETUP
================================================================================
* Printer Model : TVS RP 3200 Series (or any standard 80mm ESC/POS USB printer)
* Paper Roll    : 80mm (3-inch) Thermal Paper
* Detection     : The printer status indicator in the top-right ribbon displays:
                  - GREEN (● 80mm Ready): Printer connected and ready.
                  - GRAY  (○ Disconnected): USB cable unplugged or printer turned OFF.
* Virtual PDF/XPS printers are automatically blocked to maintain receipt formatting.
